## 🕋 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
**Navoiy-Terra** is a high-precision digital corpus and the interdisciplinary knowledge engine of the AIUZ Terra ecosystem. It provides the **Semantic Foundation** for the national tourism strategy **"Women Made Central Asia" (WMCA)**. 

While WMCA presents the living history and human connection for global travelers, Navoiy-Terra documents the verified intellectual DNA—texts, terminologies, and scientific lineages—that makes such a narrative possible.

## 🕸️ KNOWLEDGE ARCHITECTURE: THE ANCESTRY LAYER (2026 Update)
We have integrated a critical layer of "Ancestry Data". This collection proves that Alisher Navoi’s work was the pinnacle of a continuous, centuries-long scientific and ethical chain in Central Asia.

### Core Scholarly Additions:
* **The Global Context**: S. Frederick Starr’s *Lost Enlightenment*. Positions Central Asia as a global intellectual center.
* **The First Renaissance**: M. Khayrullaev’s research on the 9th–12th century cultural surge.
* **Biographical Database**: Collections including *Forty Scholars of Central Asia* (1961), *Great Scholars of Uzbekistan* (1943), and *Buyuk Allomalarimiz* for AI-graph construction.

## 🏛️ REPOSITORY STRUCTURE
- `/texts/` — Primary lyric and didactic works by Alisher Navoi (Ghazals, Lisonut-Tayr).
- `/science/` — Medicine (Tabobat) and musicology of the Timurid era.
- `/ancestry/` — (NEW) Historical records of the scholars who shaped the region's intellectual DNA.
- `/analysis/` — Semantic networks and linguistic lexicons for AI models.
- `/docs/` — Technical manuals, designs, and Zenodo instructions.
